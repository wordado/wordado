Privacy policy

Last updated 27 September 2026

Wordado is an app for learning English words. This page says what personal data Wordado collects, why, who handles it for us, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it. We collect as little as the app needs, we do not sell data, and there is no advertising or tracking.

Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is [Controller name], [Address]. For anything about your data, write to [Contact email].

What we collect

You can study without an account. With an account, we keep:

  • Your email address, to sign you in with a one-time code.
  • Your name and profile picture, and the sign-in tokens Google gives us, only if you sign in with Google.
  • Your country, which you choose when you sign up. We ask for your year of birth to check your age, but that check happens on your device, and we do not keep the year.
  • Your learning history: each answer (the word, the exercise, whether you knew it, how long you took, the time and your time zone), your progress, the days you studied, and your settings, such as your level and daily goal.
  • Your sign-in sessions: for each device you sign in on, the IP address and browser it used.
  • Reminders, only if you turn them on: the address your browser gives us for sending them, the time you chose, your time zone and your interface language.
  • Problems you report about a word, with your note.

To keep sign-in safe, we also keep a record of each sign-in code we email (the address and the time) and short counters per IP address, which limit how often codes can be requested.

What stays on your device

Wordado works offline, so your browser keeps a copy of your study data, the word lists, the audio you have played and your interface language. Without an account, your study data stays on your device. It reaches us only if you later create an account and choose to keep it.

Cookies

We use one cookie, and only when you are signed in: it keeps you signed in. It is essential for the service, so we do not ask for consent. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and no third-party tracking scripts.

Why we use your data

  • To provide Wordado (your account, sign-in, saving and syncing your progress, and the choice of words to study): this is necessary to perform our agreement with you.
  • To keep the service secure (sessions, limits on sign-in codes, blocking abuse): this is our legitimate interest in running a safe service.
  • To send reminders: your consent, which you give by turning them on and can withdraw by turning them off.

Who handles your data for us

  • Cloudflare runs the app and its files. Every request passes through its global network, which processes your IP address.
  • Neon hosts our database, in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Resend sends the sign-in emails. It stores the messages and their delivery logs in the United States. The emails contain only the code.
  • Google, only if you choose to sign in with Google. Google handles your Google account under its own privacy policy.
  • Your browser's push service (from Google, Mozilla, Apple or Microsoft, depending on your browser) delivers reminders. The message we send it is empty; your device asks us what to show.

When data leaves the European Economic Area, as it does with Resend and Cloudflare, it is protected by the safeguards those companies provide, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and learning history: until you delete your account.
  • A sign-in session: 60 days after you last use it.
  • A sign-in code: until you use it, and at most 5 minutes.
  • The record of emailed sign-in codes: one day.
  • Problems you reported: we keep the report to fix the word, but remove your identity from it when you delete your account.

Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • Download your data as a file: Settings › Account › Download your data (JSON).
  • Delete your account: Settings › Account › Delete your account. This removes your account and all your learning history from our servers and from that device.
  • Ask us for access to your data, to correct it, to restrict how we use it, or object to our use of it, by writing to [Contact email].
  • Complain to a data protection authority, such as the one where you live. In Bulgaria, this is the Commission for Personal Data Protection.

Children

To create an account, you must be old enough to consent to online services where you live: 14 in Bulgaria, and between 13 and 16 in other countries. If you are younger, you can still use Wordado without an account, and nothing about you reaches us.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects how we use data we already hold, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
